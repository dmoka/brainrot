# Brainrot

**What is brainrot and how to fight it.**

Interactive breathing exercises, screen time statistics, and science-backed strategies to reclaim your focus from doomscrolling and phone addiction.

## Live Site

[dmoka.github.io/brainrot](https://dmoka.github.io/brainrot)

## Features

- **Interactive breathing tool** — Three science-backed patterns: Simple Breathing, Box Breathing, and Physiological Sigh
- **Brainrot explained** — What infinite scroll content does to your brain, backed by research
- **Doomscrolling guide** — Why you can't stop and 5 strategies that actually work
- **Screen time stats** — Phone addiction by the numbers, by generation, and by app

## Breathing Patterns

| Pattern | Timing | Best For |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Simple | 4s in → 6s out | General calm, sleep, morning |
| Box Breathing | 4s in → 4s hold → 4s out → 4s hold | Focus, anxiety, pressure |
| Physiological Sigh | 2s inhale → 1.5s inhale → 7s exhale | Acute stress, panic, instant calm |

## Tech

Pure HTML + CSS + vanilla JS. No build step, no dependencies, no frameworks. Just open `index.html`.
